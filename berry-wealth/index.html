Money Log

How your Money Log works

Log salary as Income, purchases and payments as Expense, and moving money into a savings goal as Savings transfer. A savings transfer reduces your available-to-spend balance but is not counted as an expense. You can assign a transfer to a goal to update its progress automatically. Current goal balances can also be entered manually in Goals.
